Ladies League Information

Double Take-Out Popcorn Incentive

Any team that successfully makes a double take-out of their opponents' stones may receive complimentary popcorn from the bar after the game.

Limit one complimentary popcorn per team per game. Please advise the league rep when your team completes a successful double take-out.

Triple Take-Out Drinks Incentive

Any team that successfully makes a triple take-out of their opponents' stones may receive a complimentary round of drinks from the bar after the game.

Drinks may be alcoholic or non-alcoholic. Limit one complimentary round per team per game. Please advise the league rep when your team completes a successful triple take-out.

The Deneen Cup returns for the 2026/27 season. Additional details will be posted once the dates have been confirmed.
